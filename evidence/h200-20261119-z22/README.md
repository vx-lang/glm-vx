# A tensor-core `gate_up`: right values, slower than the CUDA-core kernel (#63, item 4)

`gpu/spawn/moe_mma/fp8_moe_mma16_gate_up_at`, generated by `gen_moe_mma.py`, writes the same `act` as
`fp8_moe_cvt_gate_up`:
- A block takes 16 rows of an expert's gate and the same 16 rows of up. It has 8 warps, each an eighth of the
  columns, a 256-column segment a PTX call.
- In each call a lane issues 16 loads of 16 bytes (rows g and g + 8 of gate and up, 4 chunks), then the scales,
  then x.
- x is put in f16 with a power-of-two scale a group of 128 and feeds both tiles' `mma.sync.m16n8k16`.
- The warps' sums are added in warp order, then `silu(g) * u`.
- 128 registers, two blocks an SM: TP8's 144 blocks run in one wave.

It is not wired into `ResidentGLM`; `moe_profile` gives it as the `fp8mma16gu` format.

## Correctness (`mma-check.json`, random weights, 257 experts, k = 9, hidden 6144)

| inter | against its NumPy model | against `fp8_moe_cvt_gate_up` (x in f32) |
|---|---|---|
| 256 (one rank of 8) | 5.1e-7 of max \|act\| | 2.8e-4 |
| 2048 (one GPU) | 4.3e-7 | 3.4e-4 |

## Time (ncu, cold, TP8 shapes, `moe-tp8.md` and `.json`)

| gate_up | us | instructions | warps active | issue active | long-scoreboard stall a warp |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `fp8_moe_cvt_gate_up` (served) | 13.4 | 4.42 M | 26% | 41% | 11300 cycles |
| `fp8_moe_mma16_gate_up_at` | **15.2** | 1.78 M | 14% | 15% | 13855 cycles |

The tensor cores cut the instructions 2.5x, but gate_up was never limited by instructions.
- Every warp issues its segment's loads at once, waits about 2.3 us while ~9 MB drains, then computes, three
  times over. The DRAM sits idle in the compute phases.
- The floor for its 28.3 MB is 7.9 us (z17).
- Getting there needs the next segment's loads in flight while the last one computes. Two buffers of weights and
  x come to about 170 registers, so 144 blocks of 8 warps no longer fit in one wave. So the layout has to change
  as well: x put in f16 once, so it needs fewer registers, or more, smaller blocks.

Not pursued further yet. The `down` kernel (`moe_mma=True`, 10.2 us here against 18.5) is the item-4 gain that
stands.
