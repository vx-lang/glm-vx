# `moe_mma`: the MoE down projection on the tensor cores, first GPU runs (#63, item 4)

The same H200 as z16 and z17, after a reboot (the disk survived). The kernel is
`gpu/spawn/moe_mma/fp8_moe_mma_down_at`, generated by `gen_moe_mma.py`; each warp's work is inline PTX in an
`mlir!` block:
- 16 rows a block, one warp an expert;
- the FP8 weights loaded straight into `mma.sync.m16n8k32.e4m3` A fragments;
- the activations quantized to E4M3 in groups of 128, as SGLang does.

## Correctness

`validation/moe_mma_check.py` uses random FP8 weights, scales and activations, 257 experts and k = 9.
`mma-check-final.json` is the build committed with this README.

| cols | against the NumPy model of the kernel's arithmetic | against `fp8_moe_cvt_down2` (activations in f32) |
|---|---|---|
| 256 (one rank of 8) | 9.8e-8 of max \|out\| | 2.1e-2 |
| 2048 (one GPU) | 1.2e-7 | 2.5e-2 |

The model matches to the tensor cores' f32 sums: the fragment layout, k permutation, scales and expert order are
right. The 2% against `down2` is what quantizing the activations to E4M3 costs (SGLang's precision).

`gpu/test_moe_mma_hardware.py` passes at three small shapes (`test-moe-mma*.log`).

**GLM-5.3's first 4 trained layers** (`tp-real-idle-*.json`): `validation/tp_real --ranks 1`, with and without
`--option moe_mma=true`.
- The 32 greedy tokens are the same.
- The top-5 logits move by up to 0.027, and near-tied candidates sometimes swap places.
- The step times, 1533 / 1545 us at 1000 tokens and 1689 / 1630 at 3001, are too noisy for the one MoE layer's
  ~35 us. `tp-real-mma.json` ran beside a Vx build and its times are not used.

## What the SASS shows (`../h200-20261118-z19/sass-lowering.txt`)

On sm_90, ptxas has no native FP8 `mma.sync`. One `mma.sync.m16n8k32.e4m3` becomes 12
`F2FP.F16.E4M3.UNPACK_B` and 2 `HMMA.16816.F32`. sm_89 gets a single `QMMA.16832.F32.E4M3.E4M3`. Hopper's native
FP8 tensor cores are only `wgmma`.

So on the H200 this kernel is f16 MMA with the conversions done by ptxas: 96 unpacks, 32 packs and 16 HMMA a
256-column segment. Quantizing the activations to E4M3 there buys no tensor-core speed, only lost precision.
f16 activations, with the weights unpacked once, should be as fast. f16's rounding is about 5e-4 relative, against E4M3's 2e-2 here. That is
the next variant to try.
